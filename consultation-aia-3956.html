For residents who want to participate

Have your say before 26 November 2026

The proposed second tissue paper machine is undergoing environmental impact assessment (AIA 3956). Residents can submit comments about potential effects, including air quality and odours.

Consultation ends: 26 November 2026

This is a proposal to install a second tissue paper production machine at the existing Navigator Tissue Aveiro industrial site in Cacia. The project is being assessed; a public consultation is open from 16 October to 26 November 2026. This is not a vote, and participation does not automatically approve or block the project.

How to submit a comment — tested steps

  1. Open the direct consultation page. Alternatively, on Participa select Abertas (Open) and search Navigator, not “AIA 3956”. Select Máquina de Papel 2 da Navigator Tissue Aveiro.
  2. Click Participar. If asked to log in, choose Faça login. New users can choose Ainda não está registado no portal? Clique aqui, then Individual for a personal account. Complete registration, activate the account if prompted and log in. The optional interest categories are not required to submit a comment.
  3. Under A minha participação, paste your comments into Deixe o seu comentário…. Explain what should be considered in the environmental assessment. Below you can copy and adapt a short Portuguese example. Do not claim that any particular facility is responsible unless you have evidence.
  4. In Classificador, choose the description that matches your contribution. Geral suits requests for assessment and safeguards without expressly supporting or opposing the project. Discordância means disagreement with the project. The Anexar Ficheiro upload is optional; your comment can be entered directly.
  5. Review the checkbox Autorizo a publicação do meu comentário no relatório de encerramento and decide whether you wish to consent to publication. Leaving it unchecked is not a guarantee of complete confidentiality. Save a copy of the text, click Submeter, and follow any confirmation prompts.
  6. Check your email. A successful submission should generate a message from Portal Participa with the subject Confirmação de receção da Participação - submetida com sucesso. Save it. The portal says participants will be notified when the consultation report becomes available.

Note: A public consultation is not the same as reporting a current odour incident. For an existing episode, see the separate odour reporting guide.

Example text in Portuguese

Gostaria de manifestar a minha preocupação relativamente ao projeto Máquina de Papel 2 da Navigator Tissue Aveiro (AIA 3956). Na região de Aveiro têm sido relatados episódios recorrentes de odores desagradáveis, cuja origem ainda não foi esclarecida. Solicito que a avaliação ambiental considere os possíveis impactos adicionais na qualidade do ar, os efeitos cumulativos das instalações existentes e as preocupações dos residentes. Peço que sejam avaliadas medidas adequadas de monitorização, prevenção e minimização de odores nas zonas habitacionais.

Please personalise this example: only describe odours you have personally noticed if you actually experienced them. You do not need to identify a responsible source. Comments may be published as part of the consultation, so avoid including your home address or unnecessary private information.

This consultation addresses a proposed expansion, not the separate investigation of existing odour episodes. The source of those episodes has not been established.
