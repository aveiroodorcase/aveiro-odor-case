For residents who want to participate

Have your say before 26 November 2026

The proposed second tissue paper machine is undergoing environmental impact assessment (AIA 3956). Residents can submit comments about potential effects, including air quality and odours.

Consultation ends: 26 November 2026

This is a proposal to install a second tissue paper production machine at the existing Navigator Tissue Aveiro industrial site in Cacia. The project is being assessed; a public consultation is open from 16 October to 26 November 2026. This is not a vote, and participation does not automatically approve or block the project.

Official AIA 3956 documents ↗Find the consultation on Participa ↗

How to submit a comment

  1. Read the official project summary and documents using the AIA 3956 link below. The non-technical summary is a useful starting point.
  2. Open Participa, search for “Máquina de Papel 2 da Navigator Tissue Aveiro” or “3956”, and open the matching consultation. Check that the closing date is 26 November 2026.
  3. Select the consultation's participation option (“Participar”), follow the portal's sign-in instructions if requested, and write your comment. You can submit it in Portuguese; a short, clear comment is sufficient.
  4. Submit before the deadline and keep any confirmation or reference supplied by the portal.

Example text in Portuguese

Gostaria de manifestar a minha preocupação relativamente ao projeto Máquina de Papel 2 da Navigator Tissue Aveiro (AIA 3956). Na região de Aveiro têm sido relatados episódios recorrentes de odores desagradáveis, cuja origem ainda não foi esclarecida. Solicito que a avaliação ambiental considere os possíveis impactos adicionais na qualidade do ar, os efeitos cumulativos das instalações existentes e as preocupações dos residentes. Peço que sejam avaliadas medidas adequadas de monitorização, prevenção e minimização de odores nas zonas habitacionais.

Please personalise this example: only describe odours you have personally noticed if you actually experienced them. You do not need to identify a responsible source. Comments may be published as part of the consultation, so avoid including your home address or unnecessary private information.

This consultation addresses a proposed expansion, not the separate investigation of existing odour episodes. The source of those episodes has not been established.
