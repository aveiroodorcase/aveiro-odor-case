Soon after moving, we began noticing a recurring unpleasant odour. Some episodes made it difficult to open the windows and ventilate our home. The exact first episode date is not established.

31 July 2026
Documented action / attributed account

My first public appeal about the odour

After experiencing strong, unpleasant odours in Azurva on 29, 30 and 31 July, I decided to ask other residents whether they had noticed the same problem. I published my first Facebook post in the local group “Aveiro - A mais Bela das Regiões”, describing a strong smell resembling chemicals and burning. I asked whether people in Azurva, Eixo, Esgueira or Cacia had experienced similar episodes and expressed my intention to report the situation to the relevant authorities. This was my first attempt to bring public attention to the problem, before my first formal complaint to SEPNA/GNR on 16 August 2026.

Evidence: The supplied screenshot shows 31 July; the year is confirmed by my account. Source: screenshots supplied by the author and the author’s account. See Public Awareness for translated previews and evidence limits.

October 2026

Replies, further requests and an unresolved problem

1 October 2026
Documented action / attributed account

A detailed public update on the ongoing case

By October, the odour problem had remained unresolved for more than two months. I published a detailed update describing how the episodes began in late July, how I had been recording them and which public authorities I had contacted. I explained that the case had been referred by CCDR Centro to the Portuguese Environment Agency (APA) for further examination, following my complaint through iFAMA. I also described my attempts to draw attention to the situation through the local press. In particular, I had sent an open letter entitled “What air are we breathing in Aveiro?” to Diário de Aveiro on 5 September 2026. At the time of this Facebook update, it had not been published. I wanted people to understand that the problem had not disappeared and that I was still seeking a clear explanation and effective action.

Evidence: I personally confirm the publication date as 1 October 2026. The supplied poster also displays 01.10.2026; the relative “6d” label is not used to calculate a date. Navigator issues its detailed response

Letter DF/14/2026, subject DAC-CIA-133.26, describes comparison of reported episodes with weather and operational records. 8 October 2026
Documented action / attributed account

Severe odour episodes and urgent notification to authorities

At 20:35, I recorded a very strong odour (5/5) in Esgueira, Aveiro. At 20:39, I recorded the same characteristic odour (5/5) in Azurva. The intensity caused me physical discomfort. That evening, I sent an urgent written notification to multiple Portuguese authorities, including APA, CCDR Centro, IGAMAOT, Câmara Municipal de Aveiro and GNR/SEPNA, requesting an immediate on-site investigation, coordination between the responsible authorities and information about concrete measures. The notification referred to the existing cases SEPNA 13057/2026 and iFAMA RD1835/2026. Status: Urgent notification sent on 8 October 2026. Official response and inspection results pending. Sending the notification does not establish that an inspection took place.

Evidence: Author’s report supplied for publication on 8 October 2026. The original sent notification has not been independently reviewed; personal details and recipient email addresses are withheld.
