What happened

How I started
asking for answers.

I began by asking other residents. Then I started keeping records and writing to the authorities. These are the main moments, from our first weeks in Azurva to the replies I received. The letters and case numbers are kept separately in Official Actions and Documents.

July 2026

We moved to Azurva

My husband and I moved to Azurva in July. Soon afterwards, we began noticing unpleasant smells. Some episodes made it difficult to open our windows. I cannot give an exact date for the first time we noticed it.

28–30 July 2026

The smell kept coming back

During our first weeks here, we noticed the smell again and again. I later described these days in my complaint. They helped me realise that this was something I needed to keep track of.

31 July 2026

I asked other residents

I wrote my first Facebook post to ask whether others had noticed the smell too. I wanted to understand if this was happening beyond our home. This was before my first formal complaint in August.

Brief record →
13 August 2026

I started keeping a journal

I noted a very strong smell in Azurva. This is the first entry in the journal I now keep. I began recording what I noticed so I could give the authorities something more useful than a general complaint.

16 August 2026

I contacted SEPNA/GNR

I sent my first formal complaint about the recurring smells in Azurva. I described what we had experienced and asked for the problem to be investigated. This was the start of my official correspondence.

Brief record →
5 September 2026

I opened an iFAMA complaint

I submitted a complaint through iFAMA on 5 September. It was registered and assigned to CCDR Centro. I wanted the recurring smells investigated. Registration confirmed that my complaint had entered the system, not that an inspection had happened.

Brief record →
5 September 2026

I asked the city council for help

I asked Aveiro City Council whether others had complained, whether any checks had taken place and what it could do. I referred to my existing complaints. I do not have a detailed municipal reply to share here.

Brief record →
13 September 2026

The smell reached the centre of Aveiro

Until then, I had mostly experienced this awful smell around Azurva. But that morning, I could smell it inside the bus as we passed through Esgueira. When I reached Aveiro’s railway station, it was still there. I was shocked to find the same stench in a place I had always thought of as free from it. Standing by the beautiful station that tourists love to photograph, I made my first Facebook video about the smell. How could such a lovely city smell so bad?

Brief record →
14 September 2026

I wrote to Navigator

When I first smelled that awful stench after moving here, I was shocked. I had no idea what it was, so I asked local residents on Facebook. Some said people had been talking about the so-called “Cacia smell” for years and suggested pulp production as a possible cause. I also smelled it very strongly from the train right beside Navigator’s pulp mill in Cacia. Later I found company reports about past odour problems and efforts to reduce them. That is why I wrote to Navigator to ask questions — not to accuse it.

Brief record →
17 September 2026

GNR replied

GNR replied to my complaint in September. I kept the letter with my private records. Receiving a reply was a step in the process, but I still did not have an explanation for the recurring smell.

Brief record →
20 September 2026

I asked for a coordinated investigation

As the smell continued, I sent IGAMAOT a summary of what had happened. I asked for the authorities to work together and check while the smell was present. I cannot confirm that an inspection followed this request.

Brief record →
25 September 2026

I heard from the public-health service

USP Aveiro acknowledged my earlier message about the recurring smells. I replied with more observations and my journal. I wanted the health questions taken seriously; I do not have a completed assessment to share here.

Brief record →
30 September 2026

CCDR Centro passed my complaint to APA

CCDR Centro’s letter named Navigator Pulp Aveiro in Cacia and said the matter had been sent to APA to look into possible emissions problems or an unusual event at the mill. CCDR Centro identified the facility in its own letter; it did not establish that Navigator caused my reported smells. I am still waiting for answers.

Brief record →
1 October 2026

I explained why I was still asking

I posted an update about the journal, my letters and the referral to APA. I also described my attempts to interest the local press. More than two months after the smells began, I was still looking for answers.

Brief record →
2 October 2026

I asked APA for the records

I asked APA for environmental records relevant to my complaint, including monitoring and inspection records. I sent a signed request that evening. The letters available here do not show that the records were supplied.

Brief record →
6 October 2026

Navigator sent its response

I received a response from Navigator on 7 October, in a letter dated 6 October. I keep it privately. I still do not know exactly where the recurring smell comes from and am continuing to ask for answers.

Brief record →
8 October 2026

I asked for an urgent investigation

After especially strong smells on 8 October, I asked several authorities for an urgent investigation. I received acknowledgements afterwards. The replies available to me did not confirm that an on-site inspection took place during those episodes.
