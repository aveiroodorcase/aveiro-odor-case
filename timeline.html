Dated case update

Two different odour descriptions

The case record distinguishes a severe industrial-like odour in the Aveiro area from the recurring distinctive odour in Azurva. That distinction supports investigating more than one possible source; it does not establish that the sources were different.

Dated observations

Morning intensification and a severe evening episode

The morning odour rose from 1–2/5 to 3–4/5. A later evening report rated the odour 5/5. The available record does not establish continuous odour between those periods.
