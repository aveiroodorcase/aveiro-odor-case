How it began

July 2026

We moved to Azurva

Soon after moving, we began noticing a recurring unpleasant odour. Some episodes made it difficult to open the windows and ventilate our home. I cannot give an exact date for the first time we noticed it.

6 October 2026

I heard back from Navigator

Navigator sent its detailed analysis of reported episodes, weather and operational records. It discussed an unplanned shutdown and conditions under which odours could reach Azurva, while saying it could not link its activity to most reported episodes. I report receiving the letter on 7 October. These are company statements, not proof of the origin of all the smells.

8 October 2026

The smell was so strong that I felt unwell

On the evening of 8 October, I experienced the same very strong, unpleasant odour in the Aveiro region, rated 5/5. It made me feel physically unwell. I sent an urgent notification to several Portuguese authorities, asking for an on-site investigation and concrete action. I subsequently received acknowledgements of receipt or registration, but the replies available to me did not confirm an on-site inspection during the episodes. I keep the precise observation times and locations private to protect my movements.

Supporting documents

My account, updated on 10 October 2026. I confirm the notification and subsequent acknowledgements. The originals have not been independently reviewed for this update; no inspection result is inferred.

What I am still waiting to learn

I am still asking where the smell comes from, what the investigation has found and what will change. Acknowledging my letter does not tell me whether anyone checked while the smell was present. My letter of 9 October is recorded as correspondence in Official Actions; I have not submitted a complaint to the European Commission.
