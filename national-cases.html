Across Portugal: a decade of complaints about unpleasant odours

When I started looking for answers about the smells in Azurva, I discovered that people elsewhere in Portugal had faced similar problems. Some submitted complaints, others obtained technical studies, and some cases reached Parliament or European institutions. These are documented examples from 2016 to 2026.

This is not a complete national inventory or a list of cases known to remain active. Causes and outcomes differ; a reported complaint does not prove a particular company's responsibility.

2026

Viana do Castelo · Praia Norte

In July 2026, residents of Bairro dos Pescadores complained to the municipality about strong smells from waste near municipal service buildings, alongside noise and vehicle traffic. The mayor said some services had already moved and the municipality was planning further changes; this is not proof that every complaint was resolved.

2019–2020

Viana do Castelo · Darque / rio Lima

Residents and MPs reported recurring foul-smelling discharges near the Eiffel Bridge and the Lima estuary. Parliamentarians asked the government for an explanation. The wastewater operator denied an anomaly on the day in question, while acknowledging maintenance interruptions; the source of each episode cannot be treated as established.

2026

Viana do Castelo · Meadela

In October 2026, concerns about broken communal composting containers and unpleasant smells were raised at a local parish assembly. This is a local waste-management complaint, distinct from industrial air-emission cases.

2021–2023

Figueira da Foz

The municipality commissioned IDAD to study odours through resident questionnaires, source inventory and field measurements. An April 2023 council minute indicated that the final report had not yet been delivered. Its eventual outcome remains unverified in this review.

2022

Ansião / Pombal (distrito de Leiria)

Residents of several villages described intense smells that sometimes kept them indoors. The issue reached a Pombal council meeting; contemporary reports described complaints to authorities without a solution communicated at that time.

Redondos, Pombal (Leiria)

Residents again publicly complained about intense odours, saying they could not open windows or eat outdoors. Reporting mentioned suspected links to a local operation; this is not treated here as an inspection finding.

2026

Poçoilos, Setúbal

A March 2026 parliamentary question recorded allegations and an investigation concerning suspected discharges in the context of odour complaints. These are allegations under investigation, not established violations. According to the March 2026 parliamentary question, CCDR-LVT had ordered the immediate suspension of waste-management operations at COMPOSET in July 2025 for lack of an environmental permit, and a joint report had been sent to prosecutors. The suspension alone does not establish that the nuisance was permanently resolved.

2016–2021

São João da Madeira / Arrifana

A residents' petition, parliamentary initiative, technical committee and D-NOSES project addressed odour nuisance. A 2021 report described improvements and remaining occasional episodes—a reported outcome, not proof of elimination.

Why does this matter?

These cases do not point to one nationwide cause. They do show that complaints, requests for answers and the need for technical investigation recur across municipalities. In January 2024, APA stated that odours are not covered by Portugal's national air-quality monitoring network or its general regulatory monitoring framework—although sector-specific duties do exist.
