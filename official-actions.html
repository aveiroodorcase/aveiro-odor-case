Asking for help

Who I asked for help.
And what happened next.

I kept writing to different organisations because the smell kept coming back. Here is who I contacted and what happened. So far, I still do not know what is causing it.

Since 16 August 2026

I kept reporting new episodes to SEPNA/GNR

SEPNA is the environmental police unit of the GNR.

One detailed reply in September; later messages acknowledged

I first complained in August. In September, GNR told me it had checked a waste-treatment facility and found it operating within its environmental permit. But it did not say which facility was checked or when, and the reply did not explain the recurring smells.

As the smell kept returning, I sent more reports, including during strong episodes in October. I received confirmations that my messages or case had been registered, but no further detailed inspection results.

5 September 2026

I reported the problem to Aveiro City Council

This is Aveiro’s local government.

Still waiting for an answer

I asked whether other residents had complained and what the council planned to do. So far, Aveiro City Council has not answered those questions.

Brief record →

5–20 September 2026

I asked IGAMAOT for help

IGAMAOT is a national inspection body that also deals with environmental complaints. iFAMA is the online complaint portal.

Complaint registered

I reported the problem through iFAMA and added further observations. The complaint went to CCDR Centro. I also asked for a check during an actual episode of the smell.

Brief record →

14 September–7 October 2026

I contacted Navigator and received a reply

Navigator runs a pulp mill in Cacia. I asked whether its operations could be linked to the smell.

Company response received

The company sent a detailed response. It discussed possible odours from its operations but did not establish the cause of the episodes I experienced. I keep the letter private.

Brief record →

21–25 September 2026

I contacted the public-health service

USP Aveiro is the local public-health service.

Reply received

The service acknowledged my concerns, and I sent my observation log. I am still looking for answers about the possible effects on residents’ health.

Brief record →

22 September 2026

I wrote to the Portuguese Ombudsman

The Ombudsman looks into complaints about how public authorities act.

Email sent; no case number yet

The online form failed, so I sent the complaint by email. I have a copy of the sent message, but no registration number.

23 September 2026

I wrote to the Portuguese President

Automatic confirmation received

I explained how the recurring stench affects everyday life. The President’s office confirmed receipt automatically; no further response has reached me.

30 September 2026

CCDR Centro named Navigator in its letter to me

CCDR Centro is a regional public authority. APA is Portugal’s national environmental agency.

Case referred to APA

In its letter of 30 September, CCDR Centro itself identified Navigator Pulp Aveiro’s mill in Cacia and referred the matter to APA to examine possible emissions problems or an unusual operating event. This was the authority’s identification of a facility to examine — not proof that it caused the smells. I am still waiting for the results.

Brief record →

2 October 2026

I asked APA for environmental records

APA is Portugal’s national environmental agency.

Request sent

I asked for records of inspections, emissions and monitoring that might help explain these odours. APA has not yet provided the documents I requested.

Brief record →

8–9 October 2026

I asked for an urgent on-site check

Messages received, but no inspection confirmed

The smell was especially bad on 8 October, and I felt unwell. I wrote to GNR/SEPNA, APA, CCDR Centro, IGAMAOT and Aveiro City Council and asked them to investigate urgently. The next day, I wrote again, asking for real answers. I also said I was considering a complaint to the European Commission, but I have not sent one yet.

GNR confirmed my case was registered, and CCDR Centro acknowledged the message. I have seen no evidence that anyone visited during the smell itself. Aveiro City Council has still not answered my complaint about these odours.

Brief record →
Plans I have not carried out

I have deferred the possible CIRA approach indefinitely. I have not submitted a complaint to the European Commission.
