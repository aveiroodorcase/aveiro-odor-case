My private records

The steps
I have taken.

I keep the original letters and my full journal privately. This page lists only the main dates, contacts and replies. It does not reproduce the correspondence.

My correspondence

16 August 2026 · I contacted SEPNA/GNR

I reported the recurring smells. I received a reply in September.

5 September 2026 · I contacted Aveiro City Council

I asked the municipality about the problem and possible action. I do not have a detailed reply to share.

5 September 2026 · I submitted an iFAMA complaint

My complaint was registered and assigned to CCDR Centro.

21–25 September 2026 · I contacted the public-health service

USP Aveiro acknowledged my message. I keep the correspondence privately.

30 September 2026 · CCDR Centro forwarded my complaint

CCDR Centro told me the complaint had been passed to APA for examination.

2 October 2026 · I asked APA for information

I requested environmental records. I do not have a reply supplying them to share.

6 October 2026 · I received a reply from Navigator

I received the response on 7 October. The source of the recurring smells remains unclear to me.

8 October 2026 · I asked for urgent investigation

After especially strong smells, I asked several authorities to investigate. The acknowledgements did not confirm an on-site inspection during the episodes.

9 October 2026 · I asked for results

I followed up asking for findings and action.

13 October 2026 · CCDR Centro proposed an inspection

After I requested further action, CCDR Centro offered a visit to investigate odours in the Azurva area. I replied that I welcome checks in public places and prefer written communication. The inspection has not yet been confirmed as completed.

13 October 2026 · I submitted a complaint to the European Commission

The Commission’s online portal confirmed successful submission. I included the CCDR Centro letter and a summary of my odour observations. On 14 October, the Commission acknowledged and registered the complaint under CPLT(2026)03536. The case awaits assessment; there is no decision yet.

Independent context · 1964–2025

This issue did not begin in 2026

After moving to Azurva, I discovered that unpleasant odours around Cacia had been described in newspapers, raised in Parliament and discussed by residents long before I arrived. These sources establish a history of concerns in the wider area; they do not establish that every past report, or any particular episode in my own journal, had the same cause.

1964 Historical local newspaper

A local newspaper describes odours from the cellulose industry

In its 12 September 1964 issue, Ecos de Cacia discusses unpleasant industrial smells and their effects on the area, while also acknowledging the economic benefits associated with the factory. This is a historical opinion/report, not a modern environmental measurement.

2014 Parliamentary question

Odours in Cacia raised with the national government

On 12 February 2014, the ecological party Os Verdes published a parliamentary question about persistent odours in Cacia. It reported concerns from local environmental association ADACE and residents, including questions about industrial sources and a nearby SIMRIA wastewater-treatment plant. These were concerns put to the government, not findings proving the source.

Read the parliamentary question ↗
2014–2015 Company report

Navigator reports investment in reducing odorous gases

In its own 2014–2015 sustainability report (then Portucel), the company describes collection and treatment of odorous gases at the Cacia industrial complex and states that €5 million was invested in odour reduction. This documents historical industrial odour-management measures; it does not attribute the odours recorded in 2026.

Read Navigator's report in English (PDF) ↗
2015 Company announcement

The company describes treatment of malodorous process gases

A press release dated 13 October 2015 describes investments at Cacia, including systems to capture, treat and incinerate odorous gases, and reports reductions achieved at that time.

Read the company press release (PDF, Portuguese) ↗
2017 Regional news report

Neighbours complain of noise and intense odours

On 7 April 2017, Aveiro On Line News reported complaints by Cacia-area residents about noise and strong odours they attributed to the Navigator factory; the company questioned the pollution complaints. This records the dispute, not a confirmed attribution.

Read the regional news report ↗
2018 Company report

Navigator publishes its own estimates of odour reduction

The company's 2018 sustainability report discusses odour-reduction measures in Aveiro and presents modelled before-and-after estimates. These indicate efforts to reduce odour impacts, not that all odours ceased or that the 2026 situation has been resolved.

Read the 2018 company report (PDF, English) ↗
2022–2025 Public discussions

Residents and visitors continue discussing different smells

Threads on Reddit describe odours around Cacia and elsewhere in Aveiro; different contributors report different experiences and suggest different explanations. They are useful testimony about public awareness, but not verified measurements or source attribution.
