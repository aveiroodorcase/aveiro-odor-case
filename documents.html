What the available summary says

The case summary reports that, under certain meteorological conditions, odours from the Cacia industrial complex may reach Azurva. It also states that the origin of most reported episodes was not confirmed.

This is a paraphrase of a conversation summary, not a quotation or verified translation of the letter. The original is needed to check its exact wording, technical basis and qualifications.

The summary does not establish the source of a particular observation, the origin of all recurring odours, a pollutant concentration or a regulatory breach.
