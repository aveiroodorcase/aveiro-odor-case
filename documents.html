My correspondence

Since August 2026, I have reported the recurring smells to environmental, municipal and public-health authorities. I have also asked an industrial operator for information.

Some messages were acknowledged or forwarded to another authority. I am still waiting for clear findings about the source of the recurring smell and what will be done about it.

I keep the original letters privately. This website does not reproduce incoming correspondence or confidential documents.

16 August 2026 · My first official complaint

I reported the recurring smells to SEPNA/GNR. Case references are retained in my private correspondence.

5 September 2026 · I contacted Aveiro City Council

I asked the municipality to investigate and explain what action it could take.

5 September 2026 · My environmental complaint

I submitted a complaint through iFAMA. Reference: RD1835/2026.

September 2026 · A reply from GNR

GNR replied to my complaint. I have not received a confirmed explanation of the recurring smell.

30 September 2026 · CCDR Centro referred the case

CCDR Centro informed me that the matter had been forwarded to APA for examination. This is not proof that an inspection has taken place.

6 October 2026 · A reply from an industrial operator

I received a response concerning the smells and possible sources. I keep the letter privately. The origin of the episodes remains unconfirmed.

8 October 2026 · My urgent notification

After strong smells in Esgueira and Azurva, I asked several authorities for urgent investigation. I reported physical discomfort.

9 October 2026 · I requested action

I asked for findings and practical next steps. I said I intended to contact the European Commission; that complaint has not yet been submitted.
