What the available summary says

The case summary reports that, under certain meteorological conditions, odours from the Cacia industrial complex may reach Azurva. It also states that the origin of most reported episodes was not confirmed.

This is a paraphrase of a conversation summary, not a quotation or verified translation of the letter. Its precise wording, technical basis and qualifications must be checked against the original before a fuller account is published.

What this does not establish: attribution of any specific observation, the origin of all recurring odours, a pollutant concentration, or a regulatory breach.
