2026-09-16 · Navigator reports an unplanned shutdown

In its later letter of 6 October, Navigator reports an unplanned shutdown on 16 September and says the competent authorities were notified. The company states that abnormal operation can release odour-producing gases and that transport towards Azurva was possible under favourable weather conditions. This is the company’s account, not independent confirmation of a particular logged episode.

2026-10-06 · I heard back from Navigator

Letter DF/14/2026, subject DAC-CIA-133.26, describes comparison of reported episodes with weather and operational records. Navigator says it cannot link its activity to most reported episodes; it acknowledges some compatible wind conditions, the 16 September shutdown and the possibility of odours reaching Azurva under certain weather conditions. It also acknowledges characteristic odours from industrial and urban wastewater treatment near the railway. These are attributed company statements; the letter does not prove the origin of all recorded odours or establish pollutant concentrations.

Original Navigator letter dated 6 October 2026, three pages.

2026-10-08 · The smell was so strong that I felt unwell

I sent an urgent notification to several Portuguese authorities, asking for an on-site investigation and concrete action. I subsequently received acknowledgements of receipt or registration, but the replies available to me did not confirm an on-site inspection during the episodes. I keep the precise observation times and locations private to protect my movements.

My account, updated on 10 October 2026. I confirm the notification and subsequent acknowledgements. The originals have not been independently reviewed for this update; no inspection result is inferred.
