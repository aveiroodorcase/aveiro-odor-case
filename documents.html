Documents and sources

A long history
of bad smells

Older reports show that people were complaining about smells around Cacia decades before I moved here. Here are the documents, followed by a short record of my own letters. I keep private correspondence and my full journal offline.

Original sources · 1964–2025

The history of odour complaints around Cacia

People were talking about unpleasant smells here long before I arrived. I found an old newspaper, a question put to the Portuguese government, news reports and statements from the pulp company itself. Below are the original sources, in date order.

They show that the problem has a history. They do not prove that every smell has the same source.

1964 Historical local newspaper

Smells were already mentioned in the local newspaper

The newspaper Ecos de Cacia wrote about unpleasant smells linked to the pulp industry. It also discussed the factory’s importance to local jobs and the economy.

Read the original issue (PDF, page 1) ↗
2014 Parliamentary question

The government was asked about bad smells in Cacia

After complaints from residents and a local environmental group, the Greens raised the problem with the Portuguese government. They asked about possible industrial and wastewater-treatment sources.

Read the parliamentary question ↗
2014–2015 Company report

The pulp company reported spending money to reduce smells

Navigator, then known as Portucel, described equipment to collect and treat smelly gases at its Cacia mill. The report says it invested €5 million to reduce odours.

Read Navigator's report in English (PDF) ↗
2015 Company announcement

The company explained how it was treating smelly gases

In a 2015 press release, the company described new systems to collect and treat gases that cause bad smells.

Read the company press release (PDF, Portuguese) ↗
2017 Regional news report

Residents complained about strong smells and noise

A regional news article reported residents’ complaints about smells and noise near the mill. The company disputed the pollution complaints.

Read the regional news report ↗
2018 Company report

Navigator reported how much it believed the smells had decreased

Its 2018 report described more work to reduce smells and estimated how much things had improved. That does not mean the smells disappeared.

Read the 2018 company report (PDF, English) ↗
2022–2025 Public discussions

People continued talking about smells online

Residents and visitors discussed smells in Cacia and Aveiro on Reddit. Some described their experiences; others suggested possible causes.

Explore the original discussions ↗

How to read these sources: News articles tell us what was reported; parliamentary questions tell us what officials were asked; company reports describe the company's actions; online comments are personal experiences. None of these alone proves what caused a particular smell in Azurva in 2026.

My letters and official replies

16 August 2026 · I contacted SEPNA/GNR

I reported the recurring smells. I received a reply in September.

5 September 2026 · I contacted Aveiro City Council

I asked the municipality about the problem and possible action. I do not have a detailed reply to share.

5 September 2026 · I submitted an iFAMA complaint

My complaint was registered and assigned to CCDR Centro.

21–25 September 2026 · I contacted the public-health service

USP Aveiro acknowledged my message. I keep the correspondence privately.

30 September 2026 · CCDR Centro forwarded my complaint

CCDR Centro told me the complaint had been passed to APA for examination.

2 October 2026 · I asked APA for information

I requested environmental records. I do not have a reply supplying them to share.

6 October 2026 · I received a reply from Navigator

I received the response on 7 October. The source of the recurring smells remains unclear to me.

8 October 2026 · I asked for urgent investigation

After especially strong smells, I asked several authorities to investigate. The acknowledgements did not confirm an on-site inspection during the episodes.

9 October 2026 · I asked for results

I followed up asking for findings and action.

13 October 2026 · CCDR Centro proposed an inspection

After I requested further action, CCDR Centro offered a visit to investigate odours in the Azurva area. I replied that I welcome checks in public places and prefer written communication. The inspection has not yet been confirmed as completed.

13 October 2026 · I submitted a complaint to the European Commission

The Commission’s online portal confirmed successful submission. I included the CCDR Centro letter and a summary of my odour observations. On 14 October, the Commission acknowledged and registered the complaint under CPLT(2026)03536. The case awaits assessment; there is no decision yet.
