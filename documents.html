My correspondence

16 August 2026 · I contacted SEPNA/GNR

I reported the recurring smells. I received a reply in September.

5 September 2026 · I contacted Aveiro City Council

I asked the municipality about the problem and possible action. I do not have a detailed reply to share.

5 September 2026 · I submitted an iFAMA complaint

My complaint was registered and assigned to CCDR Centro.

30 September 2026 · CCDR Centro forwarded my complaint

CCDR Centro told me the complaint had been passed to APA for examination.

2 October 2026 · I asked APA for information

I requested environmental records. I do not have a reply supplying them to share.

21–25 September 2026 · I contacted the public-health service

USP Aveiro acknowledged my message. I keep the correspondence privately.

6 October 2026 · I received a reply from Navigator

I received the response on 7 October. The source of the recurring smells remains unclear to me.

8 October 2026 · I asked for urgent investigation

After especially strong smells, I asked several authorities to investigate. The acknowledgements did not confirm an on-site inspection during the episodes.

9 October 2026 · I asked for results

I followed up asking for findings and action. I have not submitted a complaint to the European Commission.
