How to file an environmental report in iFAMA — step by step

New to iFAMA? Follow this guide from account creation to your report reference number. Use it for odours or other environmental situations you have personally witnessed.

Open the iFAMA portal ↗Back to reporting an odour →
Important: iFAMA is an official government platform. This website explains the steps but does not send, store or track your reports.

Start here

  1. 1

    Open the portal and register

    Go to iFAMA. Select Entrar then Criar conta. You can register with E-mail e palavra-passe or Autenticação.GOV. With email registration, provide your name and email, then follow the link to set a password. The link is valid for 48 hours.

  2. 2

    Choose the appropriate subject

    Use the search box or Todas as áreas de atuação. For odours, look for the area matching what you actually observed — for example, air emissions, waste or wastewater, if offered. Do not choose “Espécies e habitats” by default: this category was used for a separate habitat complaint, not as a standard odour category. If no theme fits, follow the portal guidance or contact the competent authority. Select Efetuar denúncia when ready.

  3. 3

    Identification

    Log in if required. Review the pre-filled name, email and Âmbitos (subject categories). If you see Autorizo o reencaminhamento…, consider whether to authorise forwarding to the competent authority. Pretende o anonimato? concerns anonymity towards others, not necessarily anonymity from the platform; legal exceptions may apply. Then choose Seguinte.

  4. 4

    Describe what happened

    In Descrição da ocorrência, write plainly: what you smelled, where and when, how often, and how it affected you. You do not need to know the cause. Say that the source is unknown rather than accusing a business without evidence.

  5. 5

    Date and location

    Complete Período de ocorrência (a specific date or a period) and Localização da ocorrência. Mark a point or area on the map. If episodes come and go, say so; do not make up precise dates, times or coordinates.

  6. 6

    Other authorities and evidence

    If asked Foram contactadas outras entidades?, say whether you have already written to CCDR, APA, the municipality or GNR/SEPNA. Only identify witnesses or possible parties if you actually know relevant facts. You can attach up to 5 files totalling 15 MB, such as relevant photos, notes or previous responses.

  7. 7

    Review and submit

    Check the summary and select Finalizar. Keep the report number, usually formatted RD…/2026, and follow updates in your account. A status of “Em processamento” means processing, not that an inspection has taken place.

  8. 8

    What if it happens again?

    Open the existing case and find Novos Factos. You can add more episodes and evidence there. A separate new report may make sense for genuinely different events or locations; avoid unnecessary duplicates.

Other ways to report

GNR / SEPNA — especially during an episode

Use SOS Ambiente e Território's online form or email for suspected environmental incidents. The phone service operates around the clock. You do not need to identify a responsible company.

Open official website ↗ · sepna@gnr.pt

CCDR Centro — a simple email

Our own reporting tool prepares a Portuguese email that you send yourself. We do not receive or store form entries and nothing is filed automatically.

Open our email generator → · geral@ccdrc.pt

APA and municipality — request information and follow-up

Ask about inspections, permits, local reports and next steps, but note this does not necessarily replace a formal iFAMA report.

Open official website ↗

SOS Ambiente e Território: 808 200 520 (24/7). For an ongoing event, give its location and time.

This guide follows official IGAMAOT instructions and a real portal submission made in November 2026. Portal labels may change. Describe what you have personally seen or smelled, not unverified claims as facts. Filing a report does not guarantee an inspection.
