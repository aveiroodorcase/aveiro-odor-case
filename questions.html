What I still need to know

The questions
I keep asking.

I am looking for clear explanations and practical action, supported by evidence.

01

Where is the smell coming from?

I want the different possible sources investigated against the observations and relevant operating records. A familiar smell or a nearby factory is not enough to establish the answer.

02

What are we breathing?

I want to know which substances, if any, are present during an episode and what reliable measurements show. My sense of smell cannot answer that question.

03

Can the authorities check while it is happening?

I have asked for a way to obtain an on-site check during an active episode. A receipt or a visit at another time does not tell me what happened during a severe episode.

04

What has actually been investigated?

I want clear dates, methods and findings: which facilities or records were examined, what was found and what remains open.

05

What action will make a difference?

If a source is established, I want concrete measures and follow-up that shows whether the recurring nuisance has been reduced.

06

Who is responsible for the next step?

I want to know which authority is handling the investigation and how the different bodies will coordinate. My possible approach to CIRA is deferred; a European Commission complaint has not been submitted.
