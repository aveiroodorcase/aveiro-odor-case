# Aveiro Odor Case

A bilingual (English and Portuguese) independent resident-led website documenting recurring unpleasant odours in Azurva and the wider Aveiro area, a timeline of official correspondence, possible sources (not established), technical context, and ways for residents to report observations.

## Public data
The public odour log presents aggregated observations. The detailed original journal and associated meteorological data remain separate from the public website. Community reports are labelled as unverified observations, not official measurements.

## Publication
GitHub Pages publishes the default branch from the repository root. The site uses static HTML/CSS/JavaScript.

## Case status (updated 5 November 2026)
- The iFAMA complaint is registered as RD1835/2026, with subsequent supplementary information.
- A complaint to the European Commission was submitted on 13 October 2026 and registered on 14 October 2026 as CPLT(2026)03536. Registration is not a finding of infringement or confirmation of a formal infringement procedure.
- A petition to the European Parliament (PETI) is being considered; no submission is confirmed.
- IDAD responded on 30 October 2026 recommending a territory-wide technical diagnosis initiated in principle by a competent public body; ZERO responded on 2 November 2026.
- The origins of the recurring odours are not established. No operator is presented as the proven cause of all odour episodes.
- Engagement with CIRA is deferred indefinitely.

See official-actions.html and the dated timeline for detail and source context.
