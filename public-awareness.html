My public appeals

Speaking up
about the smell.

How I tried to bring attention to the problem while seeking answers.

I began speaking publicly about the smell on 31 July, before my first formal complaint. I wanted to know whether other residents had experienced it too, and to keep attention on a problem that had not gone away.

These short accounts describe three Facebook publications without reproducing profile details, identifiable previews or the original wording.

Public appeal31 July 2026

I asked other residents if they had noticed it too

I had noticed repeated unpleasant smells in late July. I asked people in a local online group whether they had experienced a similar problem and said I intended to contact the authorities.

This was my first public appeal, before my formal complaint to SEPNA/GNR on 16 August.
