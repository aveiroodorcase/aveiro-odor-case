Speaking publicly

I asked others
if they noticed it too.

I began speaking publicly on 31 July, before my first formal complaint. I wanted to know whether other residents had noticed the smell too. Older posts and discussions showed me that odours had been a concern here before we arrived, although they may not all have the same cause.

31 July 2026

I asked other residents

After repeated smells in late July, I posted in a local Facebook group. I asked whether others had noticed the problem and said I intended to contact the authorities.

13 September 2026

I shared a video

I made a video about the smell and how it affected me. I asked other residents to share their experiences. I wanted people to see that the problem was continuing.

1 October 2026

I shared what had happened so far

I wrote about my journal, the authorities I had contacted and the referral to APA. I wanted people to know that the smell had not gone away.

I kept sharing updates

I also use comments under an existing post to share new observations. The weekly log brings those observations together here.
