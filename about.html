My story

About me
and this website.

We moved to Azurva in July 2026. Soon after, my husband and I began noticing a strong, unpleasant smell that kept coming back.

This is not just a slightly unpleasant smell. At its worst, it reeks of rotten cabbage, sewage or rotten eggs, sometimes with a burnt or chemical-like note. It can be so strong that we cannot open our windows or comfortably stay outside, and on some occasions it has made me feel physically unwell. On some days, it returns more than once.

It has not only happened in Azurva. I have also noticed unpleasant smells around Eixo, including near the river, in Aveiro and Esgueira, and while passing through Cacia by train. I do not know whether all these smells have the same source.

After reading older posts and discussions from local residents, I realised that unpleasant odours had been a concern in this area long before we arrived.

I started keeping a record, contacting the authorities and asking questions. But the smell continues, and I still don't have a clear answer about its source.

I created this website to share my experience, the steps I've taken and the responses I've received.

I want to find out where the smell comes from and see real action to stop it.

For personal safety, I have chosen not to share identifying details. The public odour log is also less detailed than my original records.
