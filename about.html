My story

I just want to be able
to open my windows.

This is my story of trying to understand a recurring smell and get back to everyday life.

My husband and I moved to Azurva in July 2026. We didn't expect that something as simple as opening our windows would sometimes become a problem.

Soon after moving in, we started noticing a strong, unpleasant smell outside. Sometimes it disappeared quickly. Other times, it was so intense that we had to keep the windows closed.

We both smell it. And we still don't know exactly where it comes from.

So I started writing everything down.

At first, I simply wanted to understand what was happening. I noted the dates, times and how strong the smell was. I also began checking the wind to see whether there was a pattern.

Then I started contacting the authorities. I sent complaints, received reference numbers and waited for answers. But the smell kept coming back.

Why I made this website

I wanted to put the whole story in one place: what we've experienced, what I've recorded, who I've contacted and what they've told me.

I also want people to see that behind all these official letters and case numbers, there are people trying to live their everyday lives.

Most of all, I want to know where the smell is coming from, whether it could affect our health and what can be done to stop it.

I don't know which source is responsible for each episode. I want that question investigated, rather than assume the answer.

Sharing the story, keeping our lives private

I share what has happened without publishing our names, contact details, home address or routes. My detailed journal stays private. The public log combines observations into weekly regional summaries, so it shows the recurring problem without tracing my movements.

If you want to understand how I keep the records, the explanation is available alongside the log.
