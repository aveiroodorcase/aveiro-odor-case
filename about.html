Purpose & methodology

An independent public record.

This project documents one resident’s case concerning recurring odours in Azurva and the Aveiro region. It seeks an evidence-based investigation and an accountable response.

Scope

This is a public dossier of a documented case. It is not a crowd-reporting platform, an official monitoring service or a representative survey of the population. No accounts, complaint forms or submissions from visitors are collected.

The site uses English summaries of source material originally recorded in Russian or described in Portuguese correspondence. Subjective descriptions retain qualifiers such as “-like”. The title uses “Odor”; the text uses “odour”.

How records were assembled

The available reviewed workbook contributes 34 structured observations from 13 August to 21 September 2026. Nine selected observations from dated conversation records extend the public extract through 8 October. The July account is retrospective and excluded from log totals.

The full journal for the intervening period is unavailable here. Counts are calculated from the published extract: 43 observations, comprising 38 odour observations and five no-odour controls. They are not a count of independently verified emission events, complete monitoring days or all episodes experienced.

Source IDs beginning W identify workbook rows; U identifies later case updates. Records are ordered by date; broad time periods do not imply exact chronology within a period. Portugal local time (Europe/Lisbon) is the source convention. Continuous duration is not inferred between observations.

What the observations can establish

They record a reported sensory experience, its date, broad area and subjective intensity. They do not measure pollutant concentrations, identify a chemical substance, establish a medical cause or demonstrate non-compliance.

Wind data in the private workbook have varied provenance and have not been reverified for this edition. They are omitted from the public extract. Wind direction alone cannot establish the origin of an odour.

Correspondence and attribution

Verified originals, conversation summaries and pending actions must remain distinguishable. The Navigator response is represented only through a qualified case-summary paraphrase until the original is available. No finding attributes the recorded episodes to Navigator or any other named operator.

Agency references are published only to the extent recoverable from accessible case history. No missing registration number, inspection outcome or date is invented. CIRA submission on 8 October remains unconfirmed.

Privacy and publication

The public extract excludes personal names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, signatures, precise movements, household information and health details. Locations are reduced to Azurva or the Aveiro area, and exact times to broad periods.

No analytics, third-party fonts, maps, cookies or browser storage are added by this site. GitHub hosts the files and applies its own service practices. Links to official websites lead to external services.

Only sanitised release files belong in the public repository. Raw workbooks, private source files and unredacted correspondence must stay outside it. Existing repository history is separate from this site’s public content and may require its own privacy review.

Updates and corrections

This edition is dated 8 October 2026. Future updates should replace qualified summaries with checked originals, restore missing journal coverage, and change action statuses only when supported by dated evidence. Corrections should preserve the distinction between an observation and an attribution.
