The person behind the record

A resident.
Looking for clear answers.

I live in Azurva, in the Aveiro region of Portugal. This is my account of a recurring problem and my effort to understand it.

My story

It started with everyday life at home.

We moved to Azurva in July 2026. Soon after, we began noticing a recurring unpleasant odour. Sometimes it was faint. Other times, it was so strong that we could not open our windows to ventilate our home.

I started keeping a record of when it happened. Then I began contacting the authorities, hoping to understand where the smell was coming from and what could be done.

Months later, I am still looking for clear answers.

I publish this account as a resident without naming myself or disclosing my home address. The purpose is to make the evidence and the process visible while protecting personal privacy.

What I want this project to achieve

I want to know where the odours are coming from, what substances are present, and what can be done to reduce the nuisance. I want any measures to be followed by evidence that they work.

This site brings my experience, observations, correspondence and unresolved questions together. It is a personal case record, not a population survey or an official monitoring service.

How the journal works

The current public journal contains 98 rows from 13 August through 8 October 2026: 65 odour observations and 33 controls recorded as absence of the recurring odour. One control reports a different smoke-like odour. The July account is retrospective and excluded from these totals.

Each W identifier corresponds to a row in the monitoring journal. Earlier conversation updates have been reconciled with those rows to avoid duplicates. Repeated checks during a continuing episode are not automatically independent events.

Descriptions are translated into English with sensory qualifiers preserved. Intensity is subjective: 0 means the recurring odour was absent, and 1–5 describes increasing perceived strength. A smell described as “chemical-like” or “sulphurous” does not identify a chemical or prove exposure to a particular substance.

Dates follow Portugal local time, Europe/Lisbon. Personal observation times are grouped into broad periods. Missing end times are not guessed, and gaps between entries do not imply continuous odour or clean air.

How weather is presented

Station values are reproduced as reported in the journal, with source, UTC measurement time, a broad time-gap band and the journal’s reliability label. That label is not independent verification of the underlying reading. IPMA Aveiro (Universidade) conditions may differ from those at the observation area.

Earlier mixed forecasts and reconstructed weather values are withheld pending source review. An undetermined direction is not interpreted as calm. Older measurements are not presented as contemporaneous readings. Wind direction alone cannot identify an odour source.

Observation, documented fact and hypothesis

An observation records a reported sensory experience. A documented fact is supported by an identified source, within that source’s scope. A hypothesis is a question to investigate. These are kept separate throughout the site.

Original Navigator and agency letters, sent-email screenshots and iFAMA records have been reviewed. Public English extracts identify what each source supports; missing replies and receipt metadata remain explicit. No unsupported date, registration number or official finding is invented. CIRA submission remains planned.

Privacy and publication

The public record excludes personal names, home addresses, phone numbers, emails, signatures, precise coordinates, routes, household details and health notes. Broad areas and broad periods replace identifying locations and personal observation times.

The working Excel and private correspondence remain private. The downloadable CSV is a separate English public extract. Future document downloads require both factual review and effective redaction of content and metadata.

No analytics, cookies, third-party fonts or visitor forms are added by this site. GitHub hosts the static files. External source links lead to other services; some original records are in Portuguese.

Updates and corrections

The journal is maintained as a living record. Corrections should preserve source IDs, explain material changes and distinguish a revised observation from a new episode. Action statuses change only when supported by evidence.
