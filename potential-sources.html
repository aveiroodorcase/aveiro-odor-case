01 / Pulp production · Cacia

Navigator’s Cacia industrial complex

What the published information says

Navigator describes a pulp-producing complex in Cacia with biomass energy facilities. APA lists AIA process 3939 for environmental improvement projects at Navigator Pulp Aveiro. The register entry does not itself establish the cause of this resident’s odour observations.

Company facility description ↗APA process 3939 ↗

What needs investigating

Can specific reported episodes be matched to operating events, emission sources and contemporaneous meteorology? What do the original company response and relevant monitoring records actually establish?

The original response dated 6 October has been reviewed and is summarised in the document register. No conclusion here attributes every odour, or any specific logged episode, to Navigator.

02 / Foundry · Cacia / Aveiro

Funfrap / Tupy

What the published information says

The Portuguese Foundry Association lists Funfrap in Cacia, Aveiro, and links to Tupy. Tupy’s own company information identifies a manufacturing presence in Aveiro. These sources establish the facility’s existence and industrial context.

Foundry association listing ↗Tupy company information ↗

What needs investigating

Which processes and emission points operate at the facility, what substances are monitored, and could verified operating and weather records explain any of the different odour descriptions?

A candidate for examination, not an attributed source. This site does not infer a particular chemical, process failure or regulatory breach from a sensory description.

03 / Waste treatment · Aveiro / Eirol

ERSUC’s Aveiro treatment facilities

What the published information says

ERSUC’s public document library lists environmental documentation for its Aveiro unit. A regional municipal publication identifies the mechanical-biological treatment unit and landfill at Eirol.

Operator environmental documentation ↗Regional municipal publication ↗

What needs investigating

Do operating records, odour-control information and local meteorology support or rule out a connection with any reported episode? A regional inventory should examine facilities consistently rather than select one operator in advance.

Documented facility presence does not establish transport to Azurva or responsibility for any logged smell.

More than one smell may need more than one explanation.

My private journal includes different odour descriptions, including a heavy industrial-like smell and the recurring distinctive smell. That difference is a reason to examine multiple possibilities. It does not prove different sources.

This is an initial, non-exhaustive inventory. Other sources should be added only with a clear factual basis and a stated investigation question.
