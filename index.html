My life in Azurva

Living with an
unexplained smell.

Azurva, Aveiro, Portugal · Since July 2026

My husband and I moved to Azurva in July 2026. Soon after, we began noticing a strong, unpleasant smell that kept coming back.

Sometimes it is faint. Sometimes it is so strong that we cannot open our windows. I sometimes notice it almost every day, or more than once in a day. It affects how I use my home and spend time outside.

My husband smells it too and has witnessed many of the episodes.

Where does the smell come from, what are we breathing, and what will be done about it?

I am still looking for answers.

I want the authorities to find out what is causing the smell and do something about it.

Why I made this website

After reading older Facebook posts and discussions from local residents, I realised that unpleasant odours had been a concern here long before we arrived. That does not mean every report has the same cause.

I started keeping a record, asking other residents about their experiences and contacting the authorities. I received replies and case numbers, but the smell continued.

I made this website to bring the story together: what we have experienced, what I have done and what I have been told.

What happened next?

I began speaking publicly on 31 July and sent my first formal complaint in August. Since then, I have contacted several authorities and Navigator. After especially strong episodes in October, I asked for an urgent check while the smell was present.

I still do not know exactly where the smell comes from. Here you can follow what happened next and see the questions that remain.
