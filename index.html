A resident’s story

Living with an
unexplained odour

Azurva, Aveiro, Portugal — Since July 2026

We moved to Azurva in July 2026. Soon after, we began noticing a recurring unpleasant odour. Sometimes it was faint. Other times, it was so strong that we could not open our windows to ventilate our home.

I started keeping a record of when it happened. Then I began contacting the authorities, hoping to understand where the smell was coming from and what could be done.

Months later, I am still looking for clear answers.

Where is the odour coming from, what are we breathing, and what is being done about it?

Making the process visible

Why I created this website

Over the past months, information about this case has become scattered across emails, complaints, reference numbers, official replies, spreadsheets and documents.

I created this website to bring everything together in one place and make the process public and transparent.

Here you can see what I experienced, what I recorded, whom I contacted, what responses I received, what happened next — and what remains unresolved.

Everyday life

What I have experienced

The smell comes and goes. A clear interval can be followed by another episode. The journal records both, including moments when the recurring smell is absent.

My descriptions reflect what I could smell and how strong it felt. They do not identify a substance or prove where it came from.
