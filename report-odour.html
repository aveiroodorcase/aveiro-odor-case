For residents · Aveiro region

How to report a bad smell

You don't need to carry out an investigation, identify a factory or know Portuguese to explain what you personally experienced. You can send a short email to CCDR Centro, a regional public authority.

This is not a complaint submission service. This page never collects or sends your information. It simply helps you prepare an email in Portuguese. You decide whether to send it from your own email app.

1. Describe what you noticed

Enter only details you are comfortable sharing. Approximate locations are enough; avoid a home address. Use your own experiences, not assumptions about the source.

2. Copy the Portuguese email

To: geral@ccdrc.pt
Subject: Comunicação de episódios de maus odores na região de Aveiro

Open email app
3. Send and keep the reply
Check the text and send it yourself. You can ask CCDR Centro to confirm receipt and forward your message to the competent authority. An email to a general address does not guarantee a formally registered environmental complaint or an investigation. Keep a copy of the email and any case number or reply.
If you prefer the dedicated official reporting route, use iFAMA, which routes environmental complaints to participating authorities. That portal may require more steps.
Privacy: This page does not save your answers on this website or transmit them to Aveiro Odor Case. Your email provider and the recipient authority will process information you choose to send. Do not include identification documents, phone numbers or exact home addresses unless genuinely necessary.
