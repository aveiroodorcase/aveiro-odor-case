Reading the scale

Intensity is a personal assessment.

0 = no odour; 1 = barely noticeable; 2 = weak; 3 = noticeable; 4 = strong; 5 = very strong or disruptive. Ranges preserve uncertainty. Sensory words such as “chemical”, “industrial-like” or “sulphurous” do not identify a substance or an operator.

Weather and duration

Context has limits.

The private workbook mixes station observations, forecasts and reconstructed weather context. These have not been independently rechecked here, so wind values are omitted from this public extract. Observation times are grouped for privacy. Missing end times are not estimated.
