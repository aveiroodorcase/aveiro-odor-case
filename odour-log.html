Data & Methodology

What is included

The reviewed journal covers 13 August 2026–8 October 2026. It contains 98 observations: 65 of the recurring smell and 33 checks when that smell was absent. The public version combines them into 9 weekly regional summaries.

The July account and the urgent correspondence of 8–9 October are separate from these totals. Missing days do not mean there was no smell.

How I describe intensity

I estimate intensity from 0 (the recurring smell absent) to 5 (very strong). For a range or changing intensity, a weekly summary uses the highest reported value. Strong observations reach 4 or 5. Another smell can be present when the recurring smell is absent.

How I avoid counting the same record twice

I compare updates with the existing journal before adding them. A duplicate is counted once. Several checks during one continuing episode can remain separate observations, but I do not describe them as separate episodes. I do not guess missing end times or assume what happened between checks.

What I keep about the weather

The private journal includes available weather information, including readings reported from IPMA Aveiro (Universidade). I retain the source, measurement time, age and verification status. The station may have different conditions from the place where I noticed the smell.

A journal label does not independently verify a reading. Forecasts, reconstructed values and readings with uncertain sources are not treated as verified measurements. An unknown wind direction does not mean calm, and wind alone cannot identify a source.

What these observations can tell us

They describe what I noticed, not measured pollution levels. Words such as burning-like or sulphurous describe a smell; they do not identify a chemical, establish a health effect or prove that a particular operator caused it. English summaries preserve uncertainty in the original descriptions.

What stays private

My master log retains precise times and locations, weather details and working notes for the competent authorities when appropriate. It is not in the public repository or downloads.
