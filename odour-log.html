Data & Methodology

The reviewed journal covers 2026-08-13–2026-10-08: 98 observations, including 65 of the recurring smell and 33 checks without it. July accounts and the late 8–9 October correspondence are separate from these totals.

I rate intensity from 0 (absent) to 5 (very strong). Summaries use the highest reported value; strong means 4 or 5. Repeated checks may describe one continuing episode. I check for duplicate records before adding updates. Missing days do not mean there was no smell.

Weather information, including journal-reported IPMA Aveiro (Universidade) readings, stays private with its source and verification status. Unverified readings and forecasts are not treated as confirmed measurements.

The summaries describe what I could smell, not measured pollution or a proven source. Weekly entries combine locations and times; monthly totals use the original dates privately. The original Excel is not published.

For personal safety, I have chosen not to share identifying details. The public odour log is also less detailed than my original records.
