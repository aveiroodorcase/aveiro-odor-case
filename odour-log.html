What I noticed about the wind

I wanted to know whether the wind makes a difference. Looking at 59 observations with more reliable wind information recorded up to 7 October, the smell was more common when the nearby weather station reported wind from due north (N).

But north wind does not always bring the smell, and I have smelled it with other winds too. These are counts of my checks, not a scientific air-quality study. Station readings may not exactly match conditions where or when I noticed the smell. The source is still unknown.

For privacy reasons, I keep the detailed records private.
