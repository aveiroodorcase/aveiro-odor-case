What I noticed about the wind

I wanted to know whether the wind makes a difference. Looking at 59 observations with more reliable wind information recorded up to 7 October, the smell was more common when the nearby weather station reported wind from due north (N).

But north wind does not always bring the smell, and I have smelled it with other winds too. These are counts of my checks, not a scientific air-quality study. Station readings may not exactly match conditions where or when I noticed the smell. The source is still unknown.

Wind data: I use actual weather observations from IPMA (Instituto Português do Mar e da Atmosfera), Portugal’s national meteorological service, recorded at the Aveiro (Universidade) station. These are official measurements, not forecasts. The station is outside Azurva, and its readings do not always match the exact time or location of my observations.

A different smell near Lidl in Cacia

5 October 2026 · Lidl, Avenida da Europa 458, Aveiro (Cacia). As we approached the supermarket, we noticed a very strong, heavy, unpleasant smell. I could even smell it inside the shop. It was still there when we left, and I noticed it again in the nearby industrial area.

This smelled different from the rotten-cabbage or sewage-like odour I often notice in Azurva. I described the incident in a complaint to SEPNA/GNR that same day. I do not know what caused this smell, and mentioning Lidl identifies where I noticed it — not its source.
