What the numbers mean

I estimate intensity on a scale from 0 (the recurring odour absent) to 5 (very strong). When a source entry gives a range or changing intensity, the summary uses its highest reported value. Descriptions such as burning-like or sulphurous describe how a smell seemed; they do not identify a chemical or its source.

Weather stays in the private record

I keep the available weather data, source, measurement time, age and verification status privately. I do not publish weather readings linked to individual observations here, because those links could narrow down personal observation times. Unverified readings and forecasts are not treated as verified measurements.
