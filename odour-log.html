Reading the scale

Intensity is a personal assessment.

0 = no odour; 1 = barely noticeable; 2 = weak; 3 = noticeable; 4 = strong; 5 = very strong or disruptive. Ranges preserve uncertainty. Sensory words such as “chemical”, “industrial-like” or “sulphurous” do not identify a substance or an operator.

Weather and duration

Context has limits.

Wind measurements attributed to IPMA Aveiro (Universidade) are reproduced as reported in the journal, with their measurement times in UTC. They have not been independently rechecked against saved station readings. Earlier mixed forecasts and reconstructed values remain unpublished pending review. Wind direction is where the wind comes from; “Undetermined” does not mean calm. Station conditions may differ from Azurva and wind alone cannot identify an odour source. Observation times are grouped for privacy; weather times are station times, not personal movements. Missing end times are not estimated.
