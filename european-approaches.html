How other European countries regulate odours

Concrete European regulatory mechanisms, with links to official texts.

These examples show existing tools and initiatives. They do not prove all odour problems are solved, or that the absence of a universal limit means no legal obligations exist.

Espanha · Alcantarilla

A municipal air-odour ordinance (final 2016 text) covers specified activities, odour assessment, inspections, limits and enforcement procedures. A local regulatory example—not evidence that all odour problems have been resolved.

Itália

Directorate Decree 309/2023 approves guidance for odorous emissions under Article 272-bis, including sampling, perception assessment and modelling.

França

Certain regulated installations must identify odour sources, log complaints, investigate causes and record corrective actions. These are not universal rules for every sector.

Países Baixos

The Omgevingswet framework addresses odours from wastewater treatment installations and livestock through specific local planning rules.

What could Portugal adopt?

An accessible procedure for recurrent complaints, coordination across possible sources, technical assessment of episodes, criteria for corrective action and transparent reporting. This is a proposal for legal review, not a claim that such duties already apply universally.

Method: distinguish adopted decisions, projects, proposals and verified outcomes. Implementation and effectiveness still need case-by-case review.
